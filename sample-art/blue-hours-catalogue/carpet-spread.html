Carpet of pomegranates

A four-tile repeat laid as a wall hanging, after a Bursa silk the workshop never saw. The drawing comes through a Seville pattern book, and each fruit keeps its own hand: the painter of the upper rows loads more cobalt and lifts late. Antimony yellow marks the seeds, and only there.

Upper register, third row. Tile 141 mm square.
