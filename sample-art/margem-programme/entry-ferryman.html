The last licensed ferry on the lower river is sold to a tourism company, and the woman who has crewed it since she was fourteen has six weeks to decide what she owes the man who taught her. Valadares films the crossings at the speed of the crossings, then one night refuses to cut away.

Not a film for the back rows of Room A: the first forty minutes are lit by the far bank. Come early and sit close.
