Cover and above: the 08:40 from Cais do Sodré, cut from paper by Noor Castellan

From the bridge. Nobody at this magazine has ever crossed the Tagus by choice. We cross because the rent is lower on the other side, and for years we complained about the 08:40 in these pages as if it would always be there to complain about.

Then the operator published its winter timetable, and the Cacilhas line was missing from the second page. Inês Valadão took a notebook aboard the next morning and stayed until the crew knew her coffee order. Her piece starts on page 12.

This is the seventh issue in the new format. Your letters on the change continue on page 8. Thirty-one of you dislike the masthead, and we have printed all of you.

Beatriz Noronha, Editor
