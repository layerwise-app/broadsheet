Sardines on yesterday’s bread

The bread must be stale. A fresh loaf collapses under the tomato and you end up eating it with a fork, which is not the idea.

Serves 41 hour, 30 minutes of it saltGrill or iron pan
12fresh sardines, gutted
600 gripe tomatoes (about 4)
2 tbspcoarse sea salt
2garlic cloves
4slices of stale sourdough
5 tbspolive oil
1 tbspred wine vinegar
1lemon, in wedges
  1. Salt the sardines inside and out. Leave on a rack in the fridge for 30 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Heat the grill, or a dry iron pan on the highest flame, for 5 minutes. It should smoke.
  3. Grate the tomatoes on the coarse side of a box grater; throw away the skins. Slice one garlic clove into the pulp. Add 3 tbsp oil, the vinegar and a pinch of salt.
  4. Oil the bread. Grill 2 minutes a side, until the edges char. Rub with the cut face of the other clove.
  5. Grill the sardines 2 to 3 minutes a side. Do not turn them until the skin lets go of the bars.
  6. Spoon the tomato over the toast, lay three sardines on each and squeeze over the lemon. Eat with your hands.

Stale means two days. Older than that, it goes in the açorda on p. 104.
