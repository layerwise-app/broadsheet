Headlines
Anton

In capitals only, from 28pt up, set solid. Headlines are written to fit the module, so the subs count characters before they write. Nameplate only in Alfa Slab One. Never in the spot.

Text
PT Serif

9.4 on 11.5pt, min. Coldset gain fills the counters at anything lighter, so nothing is ever set below 8pt or reversed below 12pt. Labels: Libre Franklin ExtraBold, tracked 10%, because it survives wet ink.

Six columns, twelve decks, three layouts a story
Black overprints
Text and rule never knock out of the spot. Press register drifts a full point at speed; overprinting black hides it.
No rich black
Newsprint takes 240% ink at most. One black plate, one spot plate, and no tint of the spot under 10%.
Dots are drawn big
Art is made for the screen, not fought into it. If the dots show, that is what they are for.
