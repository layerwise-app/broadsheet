Dora’s pick

The Drowned Regatta (1954), 9pm, Film North.

Shot in Aldmouth in a wet fortnight with a cast who could not swim. The harbour is full of water, which is now historical interest. Look out for the vicar, played by the actual vicar, losing a fight with a rowing boat at 40 minutes.
