One tomato,
five cuts of paper

The template Marta Quintal draws from for every tomato in the magazine. Each piece is cut by hand, scanned at 1200 dpi, then filled flat with a single spot ink.

1Skin. Red 485, cut first, largest
2Flesh. Orange 171, cut 9 mm inside the skin
3Five seed pockets. Skin red overprinted once
4Thirty seeds. Punched, not cut
5Core. Cut last, and never quite round
Ninety minutes from first cut to scan. Nothing here is drawn on a screen until the fill.
